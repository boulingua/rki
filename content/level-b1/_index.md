## What changes at B1

Three things. First, the **verbs of motion acquire prefixes**, and with them
the whole aspectual and directional system that native speakers use without
noticing: при-, у-, вы-, в-, до-, пере-, за-, об-, про-. This is the single
largest lexical-grammatical block in Russian and it gets its own unit and its
own reference table.

Second, **subordination**. Что and что́бы, reported speech without a sequence
of tenses, relative clauses with кото́рый agreeing in gender and number with
its antecedent but taking its case from its own clause. Learners who have
survived A2 on parataxis have to give it up here.

Third, **participles by recognition only**. Present and past, active and
passive participles are everywhere in written Russian and nowhere in casual
speech. B1 teaches a learner to read them and to unpack them into relative
clauses. Producing them is B2's problem.

## Reading without stress marks

From B1 the course begins to publish **authentic reading passages with the
acute removed**, because printed Russian does not mark stress and a reader who
depends on the marks cannot read anything published outside a textbook. The
transition is explicit: teaching text, vocabulary and paradigms keep their
marks throughout the course; the authentic passages inside a reading task drop
them, and the accompanying glossary restores them.

## The B1 exam

A model exam in the format of the level — reading, listening, use of language,
writing — as a page and a printable PDF, with duration, total and marking
scale. Stimuli are written for this course.

## Curriculum alignment — the `core` milestone

B1 is where the course's declared conformance level is met. `core` requires
that **every in-scope CEFR scale carrying an official A1, A2 or B1 descriptor
is implemented** somewhere in the corresponding level, and that every
(level, scale) cell with no descriptor in the Companion Volume is recorded as
`no-official-descriptor` in the course's conformance manifest. Absence of a
descriptor is never a failure; a silently missing scale is.
