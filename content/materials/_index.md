Downloadable classroom material for RKI. Slide decks and printable worksheets
are generated from the shared branded LaTeX templates (`slidegen` and
`sheetgen`), compiled locally and committed to the repository as PDFs, so the
published site never depends on a TeX installation. Audio is generated with
the shared `audiogen` pipeline from two Russian voices — one female, one male —
and published as Opus with a visible transcript under every player.

Everything here carries the stress marks and the explicit **ё** used in the
unit text, so that a printed worksheet and the screen agree.

- [Slide decks]({{< relref "/materials/presentations" >}}) — one deck per unit, with speaker notes and timings.
- [Worksheets]({{< relref "/materials/worksheets" >}}) — printable tasks with answer keys, plus the handwriting sheets for Level 0.

Audio is not collected here: it belongs to the unit it was recorded for and is
played from the unit page, beneath the text it reads.
