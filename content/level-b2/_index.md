## What partial means

A free course that claims full B2 across all seventy-nine in-scope CEFR scales
is, in practice, claiming coverage of mediation and plurilingual scales for
which no usable open material exists in Russian. This course does not make
that claim. The conformance manifest records, cell by cell, which
(level, scale) pairs are implemented and which are not, and the gaps are
visible rather than buried. The framework's own principle applies: **declare
the gap.**

Concretely, B2 is implemented for reception, production, spoken and written
interaction, and the core linguistic, sociolinguistic and pragmatic scales.
Much of mediation and all of the plurilingual scales are declared open at B2.

## What the level teaches

**Aspect, again, and properly.** Aspect is not finished at A2. At B2 the
course returns to the cases the pairs do not cover: aspect in the infinitive
after phase and modal verbs, in the imperative — where the perfective asks and
the imperfective invites — in negation, and in the general-fact imperfective
that looks like a perfect but is not.

**Participles and gerunds in production.** What B1 taught a learner to read,
B2 teaches them to write. Both gerunds (дееприча́стия), both active
participles, the passive participles and the short forms, with the register
warning attached: these are features of written and formal Russian, and a
learner who deploys them in conversation sounds like a document.

**Register.** The distance between how a Russian speaker writes an application
and how they talk to a flatmate is larger than the equivalent distance in
German or English, and it is not taught by vocabulary lists. B2 makes register
an explicit object of study.

## Reading at B2

The authentic passages at this level carry **no stress marks at all**, and
they are not abridged into textbook Russian. A learner leaving this course
should be able to open a Russian newspaper, an essay or a page of prose and
work through it with a dictionary — which is the only test of a B2 reading
claim that means anything.
