## Why the apparatus is separate

Russian grammar is a small number of very large tables. Printing the full
declension of the adjective inside the unit that first uses an adjective would
bury the unit; omitting it altogether leaves the learner with fragments. The
course therefore keeps the tables in one place, complete and stable, and links
into them from every unit that touches them.

The appendices are written for reference, not for reading through: dense,
tabular, and consistent in notation with the units. They carry the same
conventions — Cyrillic throughout, stress marked with the acute, **ё** written
as **ё**.

## The eight appendices

| Appendix | What it holds | First needed |
|---|---|---|
| Alphabet and cursive chart | All thirty-three letters plus ё in print, italic and cursive, with the letter names, the sounds, and the italic forms that mislead | Level 0 |
| Case-ending tables | The full declension of nouns, adjectives and pronouns: six cases, three genders, two numbers, with the animacy split and the stress patterns | A1 |
| Aspect-pair glossary | The imperfective/perfective pairs used anywhere in the course, with the derivation type and a use example for each member | A2 |
| Verbs of motion | The unprefixed pairs, and the prefixed forms with the meaning of each prefix, laid out as one grid | A2 |
| Stress and reduction | Where stress falls, how it moves within a paradigm, and what unstressed vowels actually sound like — the *а́канье* and *и́канье* that the audio demonstrates | Level 0 |
| Thematic glossary A1–B2 | The course vocabulary by theme and by level, with stress marked, gender and aspect given, and the unit where each item is introduced | A1 |
| Common German-L1 errors | The predictable interference errors — case for German prepositional case, the missing copula, aspect read as tense, word order carried over — with the correction and the reason | A1 |
| Assessment grid | The criteria and descriptors used to mark the productive tasks and the model exams, level by level | A1 |

## Publication

The appendices are published as the levels that need them are published: the
alphabet, cursive and stress appendices with Level 0, the case tables, glossary,
error list and assessment grid across A1 and A2, and the aspect and motion
tables as A2 completes. Each is a standalone page and, where it is worth
printing, a PDF under the [materials]({{< relref "/materials" >}}).
